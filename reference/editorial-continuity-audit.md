# First-paper continuity audit

Date: 2026-09-13. Audience: Taiwan graduate readers without assumed computing knowledge. Scope: the current first-paper route, its figures, and rendered data from the paper record. Article left unchanged. Line anchors refer to `src/pages/papers/attention-is-all-you-need.astro` at audit time.

## Editorial finding

The draft contains local definitions, but its reading order still requires knowledge supplied later. Repeated explanations change examples and architecture levels rather than advancing one explanation. Repair the dependency order before polishing individual transitions. A useful paragraph answers the question left by its predecessor, supplies the mechanism needed for that answer, and leaves a concrete reason to continue. Merely inserting “therefore” or a glossary does not accomplish this.

## Source corrections that affect the narrative

Primary check: https://arxiv.org/html/1706.03762v7 (version explicitly identified; do not assume all versions have identical wording).

- The contribution footnote credits Jakob with proposing replacement of RNNs by self-attention, and names other contributions. The draft's unqualified assertions that no sufficient public record identifies the proposer, and its author-record generalizations, need revision against the cited version. This does not establish a private meeting transcript.
- The abstract and section 6.3 report English constituency parsing, so the article cannot say the paper left all non-translation tasks untested.
- Sections 3.1–3.2 distinguish encoder/decoder sublayers, learned head projections, scaled dot products, and weighted values. Use that hierarchy to repair the three-versus-four component framing rather than claiming either selected list exhausts the model.

## Handoff to the parent

Plan the reading dependency before rewriting: concrete interpretation problem → text units and numerical data → named predecessor's operation and limitation → direct weighted access → one continuous Q/K/V calculation, including scaling and softmax → meaning and downstream use of its output → source/target architecture and its remaining mechanisms → documented historical comparison and experimental evidence. Historical material can lead, but must respect the same prerequisites. This is a suggested repair order, not a demand to shorten the chapter.

Review every adjacent paragraph using four notes: established knowledge; question still open; answer supplied; next question motivated. Repair an absent premise with explanation, not connective words. Validate terminology and case identity across graphics as well as prose. Skill validation checks structure, not whether the article now reads well; the article has not been revised in this audit.
